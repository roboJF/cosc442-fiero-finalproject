# Bug reports

Reports collected from people who have played The Halls of 7800. They are copied here as they
were submitted, lightly formatted. They have not been confirmed or investigated yet.

---

## 1. Shield doesn't stop poison

**Reported by:** a player on Undergraduate

I drank a potion of shielding before going into a room full of bugs. A few of them bit me and the
message log said the hits were completely absorbed ("for 0 (3 absorbed)" or something like that),
but right after that it said I was poisoned and I started losing health anyway. I thought the
whole point of the shield was that the hit doesn't land.

**Expected:** if the shield soaks up the entire hit, I shouldn't get poisoned by it.

**Steps:** drink a potion of shielding (the tiger statue's blessing seems to do the same thing) and
let something with a poison bite attack you. Happened more than once.

---

## 2. Lost my gold buying from Conover

**Reported by:** a player on Freshman

My pack was full (I'd been hoarding everything). I went to Conover's shop and tried to buy the
armor. He said "Your pack's full. Even I can't fit more in there." which is fair, but my gold went
down anyway, and the armor was gone from his list. It's not in my pack and it's not on the floor.
I just paid for nothing.

**Expected:** either it doesn't let me buy it and I keep my gold, or it puts it at my feet.

I think buying a healing potion with a full pack worked fine a bit earlier, so maybe it's only
some items?

---

## 3. Archivist only counts one requirements page

**Reported by:** a player on Undergraduate

I found all three torn requirements pages on the first few floors. They all show up in my pack
on one line ("3 torn requirements pages"). When I go back and talk to Archivist Dehlinger, the
Archivist says "You have 1 of 3 pages". The quest log also says 1/3. I checked every floor again and there
aren't any more pages.

**Expected:** three pages should finish the quest.

---

## 4. "Continue" puts me somewhere else

**Reported by:** a player on Graduate, seed 61 (from the side panel)

I pressed Esc to save partway through floor 2 and came back later with Continue. The game loaded
but things were wrong: a monster I'm pretty sure I had already killed was alive again, I was
standing in a different spot, and my health didn't match what I remembered. Earlier on that
floor I'd stepped on a snare and was stuck for a turn or two, not sure if that matters.

Other saves have loaded fine for me, so it doesn't happen every time.

**Expected:** Continue should put me back exactly where I left off. The README says the same
seed and actions always produce the same game.

---

## 5. Fell past a guardian

**Reported by:** a player on Undergraduate, seed 4

On one of the guardian floors (I think it was the Spaghetti Hydra one, depth 3) I stepped on a
hidden trapdoor before I'd even found the guardian, and fell to the next floor. I didn't have to
fight it at all. I thought the guardians are supposed to seal the way down until you beat them?
The notice board in the Atrium says so too.
