# How to Play The Halls of 7800

## The story

It is the year 2026. Long ago, before the YR building rose at 7800 York Road, a great tower of
learning stood on the same ground. Its scholars kept their marvels sound with an ancient discipline
the old course catalogs call COSC 442: Software Quality Assurance and Testing, and in the tower's
deepest vault lay the Diploma of Ascendance. Then the tower sank into the earth beneath Towson
University, and its testers were lost with it. With no one left to test them, the Halls below
filled with bugs, broken machines and stranger things, and the Hollow Registrar seized the Diploma.
Today a forgotten stairwell in YR 7800 leads down to the Atrium at the top of the Halls.

You are the Tiger who knows how to find bugs, and brave enough to go down. **Your goal: reach the Sanctum at depth 10, defeat the
Hollow Registrar, and carry the Diploma out through the Atrium's south stairs.**

New to the game? Choose **Orientation** on the title screen for a short guided tutorial.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move / attack | arrow keys or W A S D (walk into a monster to attack it) |
| Move diagonally | Q E Z C, or the numeric keypad |
| Wait a turn / rest | Space |
| Talk | walk into a character |
| Open a door | walk into it |
| Close a door | X |
| Search for secret doors and hidden traps | F (may take several tries) |
| Take the stairs | `>` down, `<` up (or Page Down / Page Up) |
| Pick up | G |
| Use an item | U, then its letter: wield, wear, drink, read, eat, or zap a wand |
| Throw | T, then its letter (at the nearest monster in view) |
| Drop | R, then its letter |
| Examine | L, then move the cursor; L or Esc to stop |
| Quest journal | J |
| Music / sound effects on or off | M / N (F2 / F3 on the title screen) |
| Help and symbol legend | ? or F1 |
| Save and return to the title | Esc |

In the shop, press a letter to buy, Tab to switch to selling, and Esc to leave. When you reach a
new perk level, press 1, 2 or 3 to choose.

## What you see

Press **?** in the game and then **Tab** for an illustrated legend of every symbol.

| On the map | Meaning |
|---|---|
| small figure in black and gold | you |
| gold tiger-cub face | Doc, your companion (rub the tiger statue in the Atrium) |
| robed figure | a person you can talk to (walk into them) |
| colored letter in a shaded circle | a monster: the letter is its kind, a small `z` means asleep, the bar is its health |
| larger circle with a bold outline | a boss |
| sword, shield, dart, wand | weapon, armor, thrown weapon, wand |
| flask, scroll, food, coins | potion, scroll, food, gold |
| key, rolled diploma | key, quest item |
| colored `^` on a plate | a trap you have found |
| brown planks / planks with a brass lock | door / locked door (needs that floor's key) |
| steps going down / up | stairs down (`>`) / up (`<`) |
| blue waves, green blades | shallow water (slow), tall grass (blocks sight, burns) |
| black gap, grey stones, round column, stone slab | chasm, rubble, pillar, altar |

Areas drawn dimmer with a bluish tint are places you have seen before but cannot see right now.

## The basics

- **Turns.** Nothing happens until you act. Faster monsters act more often than you.
- **Combat.** Walk into a monster to attack. Sleeping monsters are easy to hit hard. Throw darts or
  zap wands to fight from a distance.
- **Health.** You heal slowly over time, except while poisoned, burning or starving. Rest with Space
  when nothing is around.
- **Hunger.** Eat food (U) when you become hungry. Weak and starving adventurers suffer.
- **Unknown items.** Potions are known only by color and scrolls by their strange labels until you
  use one, buy one, or have it deciphered. Some potions are harmful; try them when you are healthy.
- **Equipment.** Weapons and armor may be better (+1, +2...) or cursed (negative). You learn which
  when you equip them, and a cursed item cannot be removed until the curse is lifted.
- **Experience.** Defeating monsters earns experience. Each level makes you tougher, and every third
  level lets you choose a perk.
- **Vaults and secrets.** Locked vaults hold treasure; each floor's key is somewhere on that floor.
  Some rooms hide behind secret doors: search (F) next to suspicious walls.
- **Traps.** Most traps are hidden until you step on them or find them by searching.
- **Fire.** Tall grass burns, and fire spreads. Water puts out flames.
- **Guardians.** Powerful guardians wait at the end of each region. While one lives, the stairs
  below it are sealed.

## The Atrium

The Atrium at the top of the Halls is safe. Its residents can help:

- **Archivist Dehlinger** knows about the Diploma and has a favor to ask.
- **Tinkerer Conover** runs the Curio & Circuit shop.
- **Loremaster Chakraborty**, **Scribe Irani** and **Locksmith Taylor** each have a task for you.
- The **tiger statue** is said to be lucky. Give its nose a rub.
- The **notice board** is full of hints.

Deeper in the Halls you may meet other characters too. Press J to see the quests you have taken on.

## Difficulty, scores and achievements

Choose a difficulty on the title screen: **Freshman** (easy: syllabus week, half score),
**Undergraduate** (normal: midterm season, full score), or **Graduate** (hard: thesis defense,
double score). When a run ends you see a summary and your
score; the best runs go on the High Scores table. Achievements and lifetime statistics are kept
across runs and can be viewed from the title screen.

## Tips

- Talk to everyone in the Atrium before you go down.
- If a fight is going badly, retreat up the stairs, rest, and come back.
- Keep a potion of healing for emergencies, and carry food.
- Read the descriptions of monsters with Examine (L) before you charge in.
